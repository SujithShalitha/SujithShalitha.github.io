Privacy policy

Your files stay on your PC

Last updated 4 October 2026

The short version

S Converter does not collect, store or send any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Every conversion happens on your own computer, and your files are never uploaded anywhere.

What the app does over the internet

  • Update checks. S Converter checks GitHub for a new version shortly after it starts and from time to time while it runs, and downloads new versions from GitHub. These requests contain no personal information or information about your files; like any web request, GitHub sees your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Links you click. Buttons such as Buy me a coffee open the page in your web browser. Nothing is sent until you click.

Converting files never needs the internet, and S Converter works fully offline.

What stays on your computer

S Converter saves your settings (for example your theme, output folder and shortcut) and a small update log in your Windows user profile. They never leave your PC, and uninstalling S Converter removes the app.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. To show the newest version on the download button, your browser asks GitHub for the latest release.

Children

S Converter collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
