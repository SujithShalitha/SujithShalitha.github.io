When an online converter is the better choice

On a phone, a Mac, a work computer where you can’t install software, or for a single small file you’ll never need again, a website is quick and needs nothing installed. Choose a service with a clear privacy policy that says how long it keeps your files.

Private files

Passports, contracts, bank statements, medical letters and family photos never leave your computer.

Big files

Phone videos of several gigabytes would take a long time to upload, and often go over free limits.

Questions

Are online file converters safe?

Reputable ones delete files after a while, but your files are still copied to someone else’s server. For anything private, converting on your own PC avoids that risk completely.

Is offline conversion lower quality?

No. Quality depends on the settings, not on where the work happens. S Converter uses the same kind of engines (like FFmpeg for video) that many online services use.

Do I need a powerful PC?

No. Photos and documents convert in seconds on any Windows 10 or 11 PC. Video is faster with a graphics card, which S Converter uses automatically.
