The best free file converters for Windows

There’s no single “best” converter: it depends on what you convert. Here are the free tools for Windows that work offline, what each is best at, and where S Converter fits. (S Converter is made by the author of this page, so it’s included, but every tool here is worth knowing.)

The short list

S Converter: best all-in-one

Images, video, audio, documents, PDFs and archives in one app, with presets and a Quick Convert wheel in File Explorer. Free, no ads. Windows 10 and 11.

HandBrake: best for video in depth

An open-source video transcoder with very detailed encoding controls. Converts video to MP4, MKV and WebM. Windows, Mac and Linux.

Format Factory: a long-standing all-rounder

A free Windows converter for video, audio, images and documents that has been around for many years.

XnConvert: best for advanced image batches

A batch image converter with many editing actions you can chain together. Free for private use. Windows, Mac and Linux.

LibreOffice: best for editing documents

A full, free office suite. Open a document, edit it, then export it as PDF or another office format.

What to look for in a converter

Offline

Your files stay on your PC, and big files don’t need uploading.

No surprises in the installer

Install from a trusted place, such as the Microsoft Store, and read each installer screen.

Batches

Converting a folder at once saves hours.

Your hardware

GPU acceleration makes video several times faster.

Questions

What is the best free file converter for Windows 11?

For converting many kinds of files in one app, S Converter is free, offline and made for Windows 10 and 11. For detailed video encoding, HandBrake is excellent. The best choice depends on what you convert.

Is there a free converter without ads?

Yes. S Converter, HandBrake and LibreOffice have no ads.

Can one app convert video, images and PDFs?

Yes. S Converter and Format Factory both handle many file types in one app.
