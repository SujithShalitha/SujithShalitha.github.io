Make a US visa photo: 600 × 600 px, under 240 KB

The US visa and DV lottery forms want a square JPEG of 600 × 600 pixels, no larger than 240 KB. S Converter’s Form photo crops your picture to exactly that and keeps it under the limit, without uploading a passport photo to a website.

Download S Converter

How to do it with S Converter

  1. Open S Converter, choose Images and drag in your photo.
  2. In the One-click row, press Form photo.
  3. Choose US visa and press Make the photos.
  4. The 600 × 600 JPG under 240 KB is saved next to the original, ready to upload.

Or from File Explorer

  1. Select the photo and press Ctrl + Alt + C.
  2. Press Tab for Presets, click Form photo, then US visa.

The shortcut works while S Converter is running (it waits in the system tray). In the version from GitHub you can also right-click files and choose S Converter; on Windows 11 it's under Show more options.

Questions

Does it change the background?

No. Take the photo against a plain white or off-white wall, as the rules ask.

Are the rules the same for every country?

No. Check the form’s own instructions; use Custom size in Form photo for other sizes and limits.

Where do the numbers come from?

The U.S. Department of State’s digital image requirements: square, 600 × 600 to 1200 × 1200 pixels, JPEG, 240 KB or less.
