Convert JPG and other images to PDF on Windows

Scans, receipts and photos often need to go out as a single PDF. S Converter builds it on your PC, so private documents stay private.

Download S Converter

How to do it with S Converter

  1. Open S Converter and choose PDF Tools in the sidebar.
  2. Pick Images → PDF.
  3. Add your JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP or TIFF images in the order you want the pages.
  4. Choose Single PDF for one combined file or Separate PDFs for one per image, then press Convert.

Or from File Explorer

  1. Select the photos in File Explorer and press Ctrl + Alt + C.
  2. Click PDF on the wheel.
  3. With several photos selected, S Converter asks whether you want one PDF or one per photo.

The shortcut works while S Converter is running (it waits in the system tray). In the version from GitHub you can also right-click files and choose S Converter; on Windows 11 it's under Show more options.

Questions

Does it work with iPhone HEIC photos?

Convert them to JPG first (Images → JPG), then make the PDF. Or pick PDF on the Quick Convert wheel directly.

How big will the PDF be?

The Quality slider controls it. Lower quality makes a much smaller PDF; for a smaller file afterwards, use PDF Tools → Compress.
