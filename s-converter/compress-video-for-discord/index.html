Compress a video for Discord (under 10 MB)

Without Nitro, Discord only takes files up to 10 MB. Tell S Converter the limit and it works out the right quality from the clip’s length, so it fits on the first try.

Download S Converter

How to do it with S Converter

  1. Open S Converter, choose Video and drag in your clip.
  2. On the right, switch Quality to Max size and pick 10 MB (or 8 MB to be safe, or 50 MB with Nitro Basic).
  3. Press Convert.
  4. Drag the new MP4, saved next to the original, into Discord.

Or straight from File Explorer

  1. Select the video and press Ctrl + Alt + C to open the Quick Convert wheel.
  2. Save a Discord preset once (Max size 10 MB, MP4) and it’s one click from then on.

The shortcut works while S Converter is running (it waits in the system tray). In the version from GitHub you can also right-click files and choose S Converter; on Windows 11 it's under Show more options.

Questions

How long a video fits in 10 MB?

Roughly 30 to 60 seconds looks good. Longer clips still fit, at lower quality; trim the part you want to share first.

Does Discord compress videos itself?

Discord doesn’t shrink uploads for you; files over your limit are simply refused.

Can I trim it at the same time?

Yes. Press Trim before converting and only the part you keep counts toward the size.
