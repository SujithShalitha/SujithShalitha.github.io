Privacy and private copies

Nothing is uploaded

Every conversion runs on your own PC. S Converter has no account, no ads and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection. The status bar says so: Offline · nothing is uploaded.

The only things S Converter fetches from the internet are its own updates (GitHub version) and pages you open yourself, like this guide.

Your settings, presets and history are stored on your PC only. See the full privacy policy.

Private copies

Photos from a phone usually carry hidden details: where they were taken (GPS), the camera, and the date and time. Private copy saves a copy without them.

  1. Add photos or videos.
  2. Press Private copy in the One-click row (or choose it on the wheel's Presets page).
  3. Share the copies: they're saved next to the originals with _private in the name.

What's removed: GPS location, camera make and model, date and time, and other EXIF, XMP and comment data. What stays: the picture at full quality, which way up it goes, and its colours. Videos keep their quality because they aren't re-encoded.

Converting a photo to another format (for example HEIC → JPG) also leaves these details behind.
