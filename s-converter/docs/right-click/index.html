Right-click menu

S Converter can add itself to File Explorer's right-click menu, so you can convert without any shortcut.

Turn it on or off

The installer turns it on. Switch it off or on again in Settings → Explorer right-click menu.

On Windows 11, S Converter is under Show more options (or hold Shift while you right-click). On Windows 10 it's in the menu straight away.

What's in the menu

Right-click a file or folder and point at S Converter:

You right-clickYou get
PhotosJPG, PNG, WebP, PDF, plus Email, Compress and Web presets
AudioMP3, M4A, WAV, plus Voice Note
VideoMP4, GIF, MP3, plus Shrink and WhatsApp
DocumentsPDF, TXT
PDFsCompress, Merge, JPG
FoldersZIP, 7Z, RAR, Locked ZIP
ArchivesExtract here, Extract to a folder, convert to ZIP
AnythingQuick Convert… opens the full wheel

The choices match what you selected, and the new files appear next to the originals.
