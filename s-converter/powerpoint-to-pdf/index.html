Convert PowerPoint to PDF without Office

Sharing slides as a PDF is the safest way to make them look the same everywhere. S Converter does it without PowerPoint installed.

Download S Converter

How to do it with S Converter

  1. Open S Converter and choose Documents in the sidebar.
  2. Drag in your .pptx files.
  3. Under Format, pick PDF.
  4. Press Convert. Each presentation becomes a PDF with one page per slide.

Or skip the app with Quick Convert

  1. Select your presentations in File Explorer.
  2. Press Ctrl + Alt + C. A wheel opens at your mouse.
  3. Click PDF. The converted files appear next to the originals.

The shortcut works while S Converter is running (it waits in the system tray). In the version from GitHub you can also right-click files and choose S Converter; on Windows 11 it's under Show more options.

Questions

What about old .ppt files?

S Converter reads the text of old .ppt files; for the full layout, save them as .pptx first.

Are animations and videos kept?

No. A PDF is a still page per slide, so animations and embedded videos aren’t included.
