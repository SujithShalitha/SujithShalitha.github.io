Lock a PDF with a password, or unlock one

Sending a contract, a payslip or a medical letter? Lock the PDF with a password first. And when you have a password-protected PDF you’re tired of unlocking, S Converter can save a copy without it.

Lock a PDF

  1. Open PDF Tools and choose Lock.
  2. Drop in one or more PDFs.
  3. On the right, type a password, then type it again.
  4. Press Lock. The locked copy is saved as name_locked.pdf.

Unlock a PDF

  1. Open PDF Tools and choose Unlock.
  2. Drop in the PDF.
  3. If it asks for a password to open, type it on the right.
  4. Press Unlock. The copy, name_unlocked.pdf, has no password and no limits on printing or copying.

Good to know

AES-256

The same strong encryption banks use. Without the password, nobody can open the file.

Private

Your documents are never uploaded to a PDF website.

Originals untouched

Locking and unlocking always make a new copy.

Questions

I forgot the password of my PDF. Can it unlock it?

No. Unlock removes a password you know. Nobody can open an AES-256 PDF without its password, which is exactly what makes locking safe.

Will the person I send it to need special software?

No. Any PDF reader asks for the password: Edge, Chrome, Adobe Reader, phones too.

Can I lock several PDFs at once?

Yes. Drop them all in; each gets its own locked copy with the same password.
