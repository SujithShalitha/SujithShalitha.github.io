Tutorial · free for Windows 10 and 11

Split, rotate, number and extract PDF pages

S Converter’s PDF Tools do the everyday PDF jobs without Acrobat and without uploading your documents anywhere.

Use a PDF tool

  1. Choose PDF Tools in the sidebar.
  2. Pick a tool along the top: Split, Rotate, Page numbers, Extract pages, Merge, Compress, Images → PDF or PDF → Images.
  3. Add your PDF and set the options on the right (see below).
  4. Press the button at the bottom right. The result is saved next to the original.

The options

Split: Every page makes one PDF per page; Page ranges such as 1-3, 4-8, 9- make one PDF per range (9- means page 9 to the end). Rotate: ↻ 90°, 180° or ↺ 90°. Page numbers: “Page 1 of 9”, “1 / 9”, “Page 1” or “1”, at the bottom centre, bottom right or top right. Extract pages: list the pages to keep, in the order you want, like 1, 3-5, 9-.

Good to know

Clear names

Results are named after the original: _page1, _rotated, _numbered, _pages.

Text stays text

Pages aren’t turned into pictures, so text stays sharp and searchable.

Private

Contracts and bank statements never leave your PC.

Questions

Can I reorder pages?

Yes. Extract pages keeps them in the order you type, so “3, 1, 2” puts page 3 first.

Can I split a PDF into two halves?

Yes. Choose Page ranges and type, for example, 1-10, 11-.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

Yes. Every tool works on any PDF, scanned or not.
