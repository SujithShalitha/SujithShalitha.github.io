One-click presets: Email, Instagram, WhatsApp and more

Most of the time you don’t want “a format”, you want “small enough to email” or “right for Instagram”. Presets do exactly that, in one click.

Use a preset

  1. Add your files to S Converter.
  2. Look at the One-click bar above the file list.
  3. Click a preset. It starts straight away on every file in the list.

Every preset

Images: Email (JPG, at most 1600 px), Instagram (fits 1080 × 1350), Web (WebP, at most 2048 px), Compress (same format, smaller) and Thumbnail (400 px). Video: Shrink (720p MP4), WhatsApp (480p MP4), Make GIF, Get Audio (MP3) and HD 1080p. Audio: MP3 320k, Voice Note (small mono MP3), Ringtone (the first 30 seconds) and Lossless (FLAC). Documents: To PDF and Just Text.

Good to know

Also on the wheel

The same presets are on the Presets page of the Quick Convert wheel.

Never bigger

Photos are only ever made smaller to fit, never stretched.

Originals untouched

Each preset makes a new file next to the original.

Questions

Can I change what a preset does?

Not yet. For your own settings, pick a format and adjust Quality, Resolution or Max width on the right instead.

Which preset makes the smallest video?

WhatsApp: 480p MP4, which plays on any phone. Shrink keeps 720p for a sharper picture.
