Paste as…: convert the clipboard image

Copied an image or took a screenshot? Press the Quick Convert shortcut with nothing selected: the wheel opens for the clipboard image, and the converted picture goes back on the clipboard.

Convert the clipboard

  1. Copy an image, or take a screenshot with Win + Shift + S.
  2. Make sure no files are selected, then press Ctrl + Alt + C.
  3. Click a format such as JPG, or a preset such as Under 100 KB.
  4. When the wheel says Copied — paste it anywhere, press Ctrl + V where you need it.

From the tray

Right-click S Converter’s tray icon and choose Convert the clipboard image.

Where the files go

Every clipboard image is also saved in Pictures\S Converter\Clipboard, with the date and time in the name, so nothing is lost.

Good to know

No saving first

Straight from the clipboard, back to the clipboard.

Copied back

JPG, PNG, BMP and GIF results go on the clipboard; others are shown in their folder.

Every format

All image formats and presets on the wheel work.

Questions

Nothing happens when I press the shortcut

Check that S Converter is running in the tray and that the clipboard holds an image, not text.

Can I convert every screenshot automatically?

Yes: add Pictures\Screenshots as a watch folder.
