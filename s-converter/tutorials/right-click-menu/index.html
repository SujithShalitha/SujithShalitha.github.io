Tutorial · free for Windows 10 and 11

Convert files from the right-click menu

If you prefer the mouse, S Converter adds itself to File Explorer’s right-click menu. Right-click, choose S Converter, pick what you want. Done.

Convert with a right-click

  1. In File Explorer, right-click a file, several selected files, or a folder.
  2. Point at S Converter. With the Microsoft Store version it’s right in Windows 11’s main menu; with the version from the website it’s under Show more options.
  3. Choose an action, such as Convert to JPG, Extract audio (MP3), Compress PDF or Compress to ZIP. Or choose Quick Convert… to open the wheel with every option.

What the menu offers

The menu changes with what you right-click. Photos get JPG, PNG, WebP and PDF plus presets like Email; videos get MP4, GIF and audio extraction; PDFs get Compress PDF, Merge PDFs and Convert to JPG; folders get ZIP, 7Z and a password-protected ZIP; archives get Extract here and Extract to folder. Open in S Converter adds the files to the app instead.

Don’t see it? (website version)

  1. Open S Converter and press Settings.
  2. Switch on Explorer right-click menu.

Good to know

Many files at once

Select a whole batch before right-clicking; every file is converted.

Only where it fits

S Converter only appears for files it can actually convert.

Same as the wheel

Every entry does exactly what the Quick Convert wheel does.

Questions

Why is S Converter under “Show more options”?

Windows 11 only lets apps installed from a package, like the Microsoft Store version, into its new main menu. The website version uses the classic menu, under Show more options.

Can I remove it from the menu?

In the website version, switch off Explorer right-click menu in Settings. The Microsoft Store version’s menu goes away when you uninstall the app.
