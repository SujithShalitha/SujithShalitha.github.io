Convert PDF pages to JPG images on Windows

Need a page of a PDF as a picture, for a slide, a message or a website? S Converter saves every page as a JPG.

Download S Converter

How to do it with S Converter

  1. Open S Converter and choose PDF Tools in the sidebar.
  2. Pick PDF → Images.
  3. Drag in one or more PDFs.
  4. Press Convert. Each page becomes its own JPG.

Or skip the app with Quick Convert

  1. Select your PDF in File Explorer.
  2. Press Ctrl + Alt + C. A wheel opens at your mouse.
  3. Click JPG. The converted files appear next to the originals.

The shortcut works while S Converter is running (it waits in the system tray). In the version from GitHub you can also right-click files and choose S Converter; on Windows 11 it's under Show more options.

Questions

Can I convert just one page?

Use PDF Tools → Extract pages to keep the page you want, then convert that PDF to JPG.

Can I turn the images back into a PDF?

Yes: PDF Tools → Images → PDF.
