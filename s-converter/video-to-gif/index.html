Make a GIF from a video on Windows

Turn a short clip into a GIF for chats, docs or bug reports.

Download S Converter

How to do it with S Converter

  1. Open S Converter and choose Video in the sidebar.
  2. Drag in your video.
  3. Under Format, pick GIF, then use Trim to keep only the few seconds you want.
  4. Press Convert.

Or skip the app with Quick Convert

  1. Select your video in File Explorer.
  2. Press Ctrl + Alt + C. A wheel opens at your mouse.
  3. Click GIF. The converted files appear next to the originals.

The shortcut works while S Converter is running (it waits in the system tray). In the version from GitHub you can also right-click files and choose S Converter; on Windows 11 it's under Show more options.

Questions

Why is my GIF so large?

GIF is an old format that stores every frame. Trim the clip and choose a lower resolution to keep it small.

Can I make a GIF from photos?

S Converter makes GIFs from videos. For still images, GIF is available as an image format too.
