Compress a PDF on Windows, offline

Too big to email or upload? S Converter shrinks PDFs on your PC, without sending your documents to a website.

Download S Converter

How to do it with S Converter

  1. Open S Converter and choose PDF Tools in the sidebar.
  2. Pick Compress.
  3. Drag in your PDFs.
  4. Press Convert. The smaller copies are saved next to the originals, and S Converter shows how much space you saved.

Or right from File Explorer

  1. Select the PDFs in File Explorer.
  2. Press Ctrl + Alt + C and click Compress on the wheel.

The shortcut works while S Converter is running (it waits in the system tray). In the version from GitHub you can also right-click files and choose S Converter; on Windows 11 it's under Show more options.

Questions

How much smaller will it get?

PDFs full of photos or scans often shrink a lot; PDFs that are mostly text are already small and change less.

Is it safe for confidential documents?

Yes. Everything happens on your PC and nothing is uploaded.
