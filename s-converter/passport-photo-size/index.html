Resize a photo for a passport or ID form (35 × 45 mm)

Online applications want photos at an exact size and under a file-size limit. Pick the size once and S Converter crops, resizes and compresses your photo to match.

Download S Converter

How to do it with S Converter

  1. Open S Converter, choose Images and drag in your photo.
  2. Press Form photo in the One-click row.
  3. Choose Passport 35 × 45 mm (413 × 531 px), 2 × 2 inch (600 × 600 px), or Custom size and type the width, height and KB limit from your form. Press Make the photos.
  4. The photo is saved next to the original at exactly that size.

Exam and job portals

  1. Many portals ask for something like 200 × 230 px under 50 KB.
  2. Choose Form photo → Custom size, type those numbers, and press Make the photos.

The shortcut works while S Converter is running (it waits in the system tray). In the version from GitHub you can also right-click files and choose S Converter; on Windows 11 it's under Show more options.

Questions

How many pixels is 35 × 45 mm?

At 300 dpi it’s 413 × 531 pixels, which is what S Converter makes.

Can it remove the background?

No. Take the photo against a plain, light wall.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your PC, even without an internet connection.
