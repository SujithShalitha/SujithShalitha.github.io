Features

Everything you'd use an online converter for, without the upload

Convert anything, in batches

Drop in one file or a whole folder, pick the format, press Convert. One-click presets such as Email, Instagram, Web and Thumbnail handle everyday jobs, and you see exactly how much space you saved.

  • iPhone HEIC to JPG, WebP to PNG, camera RAW and more
  • Resize, compress and change quality for every file at once
  • Save your own one-click presets, or recipes that convert, rename and pack into one ZIP or PDF
  • Rename the whole batch as it converts: Holiday-001, 002…
  • Originals are never overwritten

Make it fit: size limits and form photos

Upload forms say under 100 KB, email stops at 20 MB. Switch Quality to Max size, pick the limit, and S Converter finds the best quality that fits. Form photo crops a picture to the exact size a visa, passport or exam form asks for.

  • Photos under 100 KB, 200 KB, 1 MB or any limit you type
  • US visa photo: 600 × 600 px under 240 KB; passport 35 × 45 mm, 2 × 2 inch, or your own size
  • Videos under 20 MB for email, 16 MB for WhatsApp, or any size
  • Your ID photos never leave your PC

Quick Convert, right from File Explorer

Select files in File Explorer, on the desktop or in any Open or Save dialog, and press Ctrl + Alt + C, or right-click and choose Quick Convert. A wheel opens at your mouse: point at a format and click. The app doesn't even need to open.

  • Presets like WhatsApp, Make GIF, Get Audio and Locked ZIP
  • Several photos to one PDF, with pages you can put in order, or one PDF each
  • Paste as…: nothing selected? It converts the image on the clipboard and copies the result back
  • Learns your favourites: the formats you use most move to the front
  • Works on folders too: pack them into ZIP, 7Z or RAR
  • Pick your own keys, like F8, plus shortcuts for your presets and Repeat last

Video, with your graphics card

Convert between MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, AVI and more, make GIFs, pull out the audio, change the resolution and trim clips on a visual timeline. S Converter detects NVIDIA, Intel and AMD graphics and uses them automatically for much faster encoding.

Documents, no Office needed

Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, RTF, Markdown, HTML and EPUB, converted by S Converter's own engine. No Microsoft Office or LibreOffice required.

  • Word to PDF keeps headings, lists, tables, pictures and links
  • PowerPoint to PDF keeps each slide's layout and colours
  • Tables in a document can become an Excel spreadsheet

PDF tools, done privately

The things you usually need a PDF website for, on your own PC. Even locking a PDF with a password:

  • Images → PDF
  • PDF → Images
  • Merge
  • Split
  • Compress
  • Rotate
  • Page numbers
  • Extract pages
  • Lock with a password
  • Unlock

Watermarks for photos and videos

Add your name or logo, drag it into place on a live preview, set size, colour and opacity, repeat it so it's harder to crop out, or let it scroll across a video. One design, applied to the whole batch.

Archives and passwords

  • Pack folders into ZIP, 7Z, RAR or TAR.GZ
  • Protect them with a password: ZIP uses AES-256 encryption
  • Extract ZIP, 7Z, RAR, TAR, ISO and CAB, including password-protected ones

Private copies, without the hidden details

Phone photos carry where they were taken, the camera and the time. One click on Private copy saves a copy without them, ready to share. Full quality, and videos aren’t re-encoded.

  • Removes GPS location, camera details and dates
  • Photos and videos, in batches
  • Unlike EXIF-remover websites, nothing is uploaded

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is S Converter really free?

Yes. No ads, no trial, no account, no limits, and no watermark added to your files.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every conversion runs on your own computer, and S Converter works without an internet connection. The only thing it ever downloads is its own updates. See the privacy policy.

Can it convert Word or PowerPoint to PDF without Microsoft Office?

Yes. S Converter has its own document engine, so DOCX, DOC, ODT, RTF, PPTX, XLSX, EPUB, Markdown and HTML all convert to PDF with nothing else installed.

Can it convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG?

Yes, in batches. HEIC/HEIF and camera RAW files convert to JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, TIFF or PDF.

Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

Windows shows this for new programs that aren't signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click More info → Run anyway. You only need to do it once: updates install by themselves after that. If you'd rather avoid the warning, get S Converter from the Microsoft Store instead.

Can it remove the location from my photos?

Yes. Private copy saves a copy of a photo or video without its GPS location, camera details and date, at full quality. See how to remove location from photos.

Can it compress a photo to under 100 KB?

Yes. Switch Quality to Max size and pick 100 KB, or any limit; S Converter finds the best quality that fits. Form photo makes visa, passport and exam photos at an exact size. See compress an image to 100 KB and make a US visa photo.

Is there a manual?

Yes: the S Converter documentation explains every feature, step by step. It's also on GitHub.

Can it put a password on a PDF?

Yes. In PDF Tools, Lock adds a password with AES-256 encryption, and Unlock removes a password you know. See how to lock a PDF.

Which languages is it available in?

20: English, Sinhala, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Turkish, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Russian, Ukrainian, Hindi, Thai, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese and Korean. It follows your Windows language automatically; see how to change it.

Where do the converted files go?

By default, next to the originals, with "SC" added to the name so nothing is ever overwritten. You can choose a different folder.

The Quick Convert shortcut doesn't work.

Another program may already be using Ctrl + Alt + C. Pick a different one in Settings → Quick Convert shortcut. S Converter stays in the system tray so the shortcut keeps working after you close its window.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → S Converter → Uninstall. The right-click menu entries are removed too.

Enjoying S Converter?

It's made by one developer and it's free. If it saved you some time, a coffee helps keep it going.
